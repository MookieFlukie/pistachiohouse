Our Story

Pistachio House is a dessert shop in West Bloomfield, Michigan, built around one favorite ingredient. You'll find pistachio in our baklava, cakes, crepes and even our iced latte.

Stop in for a sweet treat with a coffee or matcha, cool off with a smoothie, or pick up a tray of baklava to share with family and friends.

Our Menu
